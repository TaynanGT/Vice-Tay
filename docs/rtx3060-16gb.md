# A six-core desktop with 16 GB: `-Machine 3060`

`scripts\play.ps1 -Machine 3060` gives these settings. Ask for them explicitly; `-Machine auto` doesn't pick them, because they were measured on one PC:

| Part | |
|---|---|
| CPU | Intel Core i5-12400F (6 cores of one kind, 12 threads) |
| GPU | NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 12 GB, DXGI budget about 11,000 MB, Resizable BAR on |
| RAM | 16 GB, a system-managed page file |

## Why not the default

`-Machine auto` takes every NVIDIA card for the 4090 PC: 12 list translation, 8 memory update and 6 landing threads on a CPU with 12 hardware threads, next to the title's own. The submitting thread, which the card waits for, gets less of the CPU, and with no windows in the card's memory the PC's 16 GB pages: in run26 below the game's working set held 4–7 GB while it read 15,000–20,000 pages a second from disk.

## The settings

On top of the NVIDIA settings and the 4070 profile ([rtx4070-performance.md](rtx4070-performance.md)), which every measured run had:

| Setting | Why |
|---|---|
| `VICTORY_THIN_THREADS=6`, `VICTORY_UNDER_THREADS=4`, `VICTORY_LAND_THREADS=3` | Six cores: the 4070's 8 / 6 / 4 gave no more frames (run25 against runs 21 and 24) and take cores from the submitting thread and the title. |
| `VICTORY_WINDOWS_BAR_MB=3072` | Up to 3 GB of windows in the card's memory (Resizable BAR) instead of RAM, as in the laptop preset. Kept under `-Safe`. |
| `VICTORY_MAP_THREADS=auto` | Pages mapped by several threads while the title streams, as on the 4090 PC; the 4070's single thread slowed the start (run15, 9.9 fps). Not under `-Safe`. |

Any one can be overridden with `-More`.

## Measurements

Built from `5c803d2` (`cargo build --locked --profile diag -p victory --features diagnostics`), every known pipeline compiled ahead with `victory warm`. Series S, windowed, 1080p output drawn at half size by the title's upscaler (`-More @{VICTORY_OUTPUT='1920x1080'; VICTORY_UPSCALER='6'; VICTORY_RENDER_SCALE='0.5'}`). One route each run, from the same prologue save: with the guard to the crowded room, a walk around it, and out before the next conversation (about 5 minutes). "Windows" are the frame log's world windows of about 5 s.

| Run | Settings | Recorded | fps median / mean | Windows under 5 fps | Steady frame |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 26 | `-Machine auto` (the 4090 settings) | yes | 5.0 / 4.7 | 24 of 50 | 118 ms |
| 27 | `-Machine 3060` | yes | **9.2 / 9.0** | 8 of 38 | 93 ms |
| 21 | `-Machine 4070` + BAR + map threads (8 / 6 / 4) | no | 10.7 / 10.5 | 3 of 35 | 84 ms |
| 24 | the same, again | no | 10.1 / 9.2 | 7 of 34 | 83 ms |
| 25 | `-Machine 3060` | no | 11.2 / 10.0 | 5 of 34 | 81 ms |

Runs 26 and 27 were recorded with OBS (NVENC), which costs both about 2 fps; runs 21, 24 and 25 were not. The two controls differ by 0.6 fps with the same settings, so the thread counts alone are within the noise; the preset's gain is over `auto`.

The new preset's settings were run with `-Machine 4070` and `-More` (this file's preset applies the same values).

## What it looks like

Runs 26 and 27 compared from their recordings, one frame every 10 s: no new artifacts with `-Machine 3060`. Under `auto` the guard's arms showed coloured noise and some faces bright white eyes; under `3060` the arms rendered normally, and one face texture was half loaded for a moment in the diner flashback. Both showed floating orange light sprites by the dormitory door and greenish skin on forearms.

## Further out

Past the prologue's buildings (the police chase and driving), the card fills to about 84 % of its budget, windows and buffer copies go to the PC's shared memory (3.3 to 6.2 GB) and the PC pages again. The laptop preset's RAM settings (`VICTORY_DSTORAGE_IO=direct`, `VICTORY_INPLACE=2`, `VICTORY_WINDOW_KB=64`, `VICTORY_WINDOW_TAILS=1`, `VICTORY_LEVELS_ON_DEMAND=1`, `VICTORY_CARD_WINDOWS_MB=0`, `VICTORY_WINDOWS_BAR_MB=5120`) did not help on this card: textures took the freed room up to 90 % of the budget (2.1 to 6.7 GB) and the frame rate fell to 7.5 fps on the same route. They are not in the preset.
